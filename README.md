Anime Recommender based on animelist 
Currently, sorts anime by # of recommendations to those watched by the user, excluding those with 0. 

Doppleganger seperate way to rec, looks at other users' animelists (taken from those who've recently scored one of the shows the targeted user has watched), finds those most correlated / similiar to the targeted user, and recommends highly rated shows in the other users' animelists (very similiar to KNN).
